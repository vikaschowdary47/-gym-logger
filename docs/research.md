# Research notes

Competition and demand research is in a separate document (Research: Voice Gym Logger Wearable).

Short version:
- Logging apps (Strong, Hevy) and voice logging apps already exist, including on Apple Watch.
- Closest hardware competitors: Whoop (screenless) and Fort (screenless, $289 pre-order).
- No cheap wrist display with tap and voice logging found.
- Plan: build in public, not as a product to compete with funded hardware.
